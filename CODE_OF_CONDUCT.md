# Code of conduct

GreenRay AIO is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews, is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- keep feedback about the work, not the person;
- welcome newcomers, including people preparing for their first certification;
- respect the licence and the non-affiliation notice: no exam dumps, no copied questions, no NVIDIA material.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sharing of real exam content are not acceptable. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, open an issue, or contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled in confidence.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and is deliberately short.
