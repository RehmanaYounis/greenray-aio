Usage:
 last [options] [<username>...] [<tty>...]

Show a listing of last logged in users.

Options:
 -<number>            how many lines to show
 -n, --limit <number> how many lines to show
 -h, --help           display this help

The listing ends with the boot record and the start of the wtmp file, which
is when the node last came up.

(GreenRay AIO simulates the options listed above.)
